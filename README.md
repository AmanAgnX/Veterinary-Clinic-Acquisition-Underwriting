# Veterinary-Clinic-Acquisition-Underwriting
# Veterinary Clinic Acquisition Underwriting

*Private Equity | Real Estate | Due Diligence | Transaction Underwriting*

> Project completed at **Lionchase North America** as a **Fund Advisory Intern**, supporting underwriting and due diligence for the **Acxtus Fund** on a live approximately **$20M veterinary clinic acquisition**.

---

## Overview

The objective was to evaluate the existing economics, operating requirements, and value-creation potential of a live veterinary clinic transaction.

The underwriting combined **property economics, operating costs, facility capacity, redevelopment alternatives, market conditions, and comparable-property analysis** to develop a structured view of the transaction.

Because the asset combined a physical facility with an operating veterinary business, the investment required analysis beyond traditional real-estate valuation.

---

## Investment Problem

The transaction needed to be evaluated across both the **property and operating business**.

The asset's future economics depended on several interconnected factors:

* Existing property economics
* Current operating-cost structure
* Facility utilization and capacity
* Potential service-line expansion
* Redevelopment requirements
* Future capital requirements
* Comparable-property economics
* Execution risk surrounding the value-creation plan

The challenge was therefore to determine how these factors affected the attractiveness and risk profile of the acquisition.

---

## What I Analyzed

I supported the live underwriting process across several transaction workstreams:

* Property and facility economics
* Operating-cost analysis
* Existing and future capacity requirements
* Service-line expansion opportunities
* Redevelopment and asset-repositioning alternatives
* Comparable-property analysis
* Market and competitive considerations
* Transaction-level value-creation opportunities

The analysis was used to better understand both the existing asset and the changes required to improve its future economics.

* Capacity requirements

This connected the financial underwriting with the operating and physical requirements of the asset.

---

## Underwriting Logic

The investment process followed:

**Existing Asset → Property Economics → Operating Economics → Market / Comps → Redevelopment Requirements → Value-Creation Opportunity → Investment View**

Several relationships were especially important:

**Capacity → Potential Service Volume → Revenue Opportunity**

**Operating Costs → Margin / Cash Flow**

**Redevelopment → Capacity / Efficiency → Future Economics**

The underwriting therefore evaluated not only what the asset was worth in its current state, but what would need to change for the investment thesis to succeed.

## What the Underwriting Evaluates

| **Investment Question**                  | **Underwriting View**                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **What are we buying?**                  | Property, operating business, capacity, and existing economics           |
| **How does the asset perform today?**    | Revenue drivers, operating costs, utilization, and facility requirements |
| **How does it compare with the market?** | Comparable properties and relevant market conditions                     |
| **Where can value be created?**          | Cost reduction, capacity expansion, service growth, redevelopment        |
| **What could impair the thesis?**        | Operating, redevelopment, demand, capital, and execution risk            |

## Investment Impact

The analysis created a structured connection between the physical property, operating business, and investment thesis.

The central underwriting chain became:

**Property + Operations + Market → Value-Creation Opportunity → Transaction Economics → Investment Decision**

The project provided direct exposure to a **live private-market investment process**, including transaction underwriting, due diligence, investor discussions, and evaluation of operating and redevelopment strategies for an approximately **$20M acquisition**.
